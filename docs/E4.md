# E4: confirmation of E3's label-free track-masked BC on fresh seeds

Session E4, 2026-11-04. Branch `art/confirm-E4` (from `art/explore-E3` at `2ef349e`). The preregistration is
`docs/E4_PREREG.md`, committed and pushed in `069e995` before any seed 5–9 cell was loaded. Nothing was changed after it.

## Result

**Confirmed on all 7 settings (7/7 PASS).** Track-masked BC (`a=track60k-w=1-st=20000`) beats B1a
(`R_hopB_bsup_neg_AMdist`) by 6.4 to 25 standard errors (contig 6.4, b3p1 7.0, pairs 7.2, k4 9.5, k2 13, k1 22, alt 25), paired over the 20 (seed, npm) cells of each
setting. It has lower D than B1a in all 140 of 140 cells.

- D 0.017–0.035 across settings, against B1a 0.107–0.449 and B0 0.149–0.516.
- Removed 0.994–0.998, Collateral 0.011–0.030.
- D does not depend on npm: one logged negative per forgotten mode is enough. Pooled D is 0.024 at npm 1, 4, 16, and 64,
  while B1a goes from 0.371 at npm 1 to 0.170 at npm 64.

## Setup

Grid: k1, k2, k4 (random forget set, n_forget 1/2/4) and contig, alt, pairs, b3p1 (n_forget 4) × npm 1, 4, 16, 64 ×
seeds 5–9. That is 140 cells and 420 runs (B0, B1a, and the method per cell), all complete, with no reruns or exclusions.
D = `tv_to_ideal`, Removed = 1 − `neg_mass_ratio`, Collateral = `retain_tv`, from `experiment.compute`.
The runner `explore/E4/e4.py` wraps `explore/E3/e3.py` unchanged. It ran as 4 worker processes with 1 thread each,
one cell per unit (`explore/E4/run_queue.sh`), and every unit exited 0. Units took 7–9 min, or ~18 min at npm 64,
where B1a alone takes ~12.5 min.
Analysis: `explore/E4/analyze.py`. The full output, including per-npm tables, mass_off, and seed-clustered SEs, is in
`explore/E4/table_final.md`. Raw results are in `explore/E4/results/<setting>/s<seed>_n<npm>/<arm>.json`.

**Sanity checks.**
1. Before the prereg, `e4.py` reproduced E3's stored k4 s0 n4 JSONs, with every numeric field identical
   (B0 0.51595, B1a 0.2588, method 0.0208). It also reproduced B0/B1a on alt s1 n16.
2. After the run, B1a's per-setting mean D and Removed on seeds 5–9 equal the method map's `confirm` values in
   `reference/method_map.json` for all 7 settings, to 3 decimals (e.g. alt D 0.449 vs 0.4497, k1 0.107 vs 0.1071).
   Collateral agrees to within 0.007. The largest gap is contig: 0.070 here against 0.077 in the map.

## Track diagnostics (the oracle is used only to score)

- π_ref forms 8–16 tracks per seed. The bad tracks match the forget set: 1–2 for k1, 2–3 for k2, 4–5 for n_forget 4.
  No logged negative fell outside a track, except 1 of 256 in one alt cell.
- Flag precision is 1.000 in all 140 cells. Recall is 0.81–0.89; the misses are atypical samples, which go
  unassigned and are dropped from the anchor anyway (16% of the pool is unassigned).
  The kept anchor holds at most 0.5% forgotten-mode samples.
- npm 4/16/64 usually give the same mask as npm 1. In 9 of 35 (setting, seed) groups, one extra track was flagged at
  higher npm. That moved D by at most 0.009.

## Notes and caveats

- contig and b3p1 have the highest method D (0.035, 0.034) and Collateral (0.030, 0.026). In these layouts 3–4
  adjacent modes are removed, so all retained mass must be redistributed over a contiguous half-ring. It is still
  6.5–7.5× below B1a.
- The test is per setting, with no multiplicity correction. All margins are far beyond any reasonable correction.
- Exploration line (outside the claims, as E3): a confirmation of an E3 finding on held-out seeds, not a change
  to the gate or the surgeon's prompt. The caveats of E3 still apply: tracks need well-separated behaviour
  clusters at each c, and this is a global edit on all negatives at once.
